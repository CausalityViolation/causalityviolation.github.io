Indie games for Android

Small, cozy games

Kawarimi Interactive is a one-person studio, run by Causality. Every game is free to play, works without an account, and only shows ads you choose to watch.

Farm Capitalist

A pixel-art idle farming tycoon. Plant, hire Farm Hands, ship the harvest and grow absurdly rich.

On Google Play

Starfurrow

A cozy farm puzzle: link three or more farm friends through 190 levels in 19 worlds, from spring to a fairy land and a firefly forest, with Pip the chick.

Coming soon

Bakery Bounce

A quick tap-to-bounce arcade game. Guide a flying pastry past rolling pins and chase your high score.

On Google Play

About the studio

I make the kind of games I like to play on the bus: easy to pick up, kind to your time and wallet, and nice to look at. No accounts needed, progress saved on your phone, and ads only when you ask for a reward.

Questions, feedback or press: dev.causality@gmail.com
