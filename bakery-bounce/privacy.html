Bakery Bounce – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 3, 2026
Developer: Causality

Overview

Bakery Bounce respects your privacy.

The game is designed to be simple and safe to use. Most features work entirely offline, and your progress is stored locally on your device.

Information Collection

Bakery Bounce does not collect personal data directly from users.

The game does not require:

  • Account creation
  • Login
  • Personal information such as name or email

All gameplay data (such as high scores, unlocked avatars, power-ups, and upgrades) is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted to the developer.

Advertisements

Bakery Bounce uses optional rewarded advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

  • Ads are shown only if you choose to watch them
  • Watching ads provides optional in-game rewards
  • The game can be fully played without watching ads

While Bakery Bounce itself does not collect personal data, AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage data

This data is handled by Google and is subject to their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics & Tracking

Bakery Bounce does not use:

  • Analytics tools
  • Tracking software
  • User behavior profiling

There is no independent tracking of user activity by the developer.

Children’s Privacy

Bakery Bounce is suitable for all ages.

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or any age). Any data potentially collected through third-party services (such as AdMob) is handled according to their respective policies.

User Consent (EEA)

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) may be presented with consent options for personalized or non-personalized ads, in accordance with applicable privacy laws such as the GDPR.

Third-Party Services

Bakery Bounce uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob (for optional rewarded ads)

The developer does not control data collected by third-party services. Please refer to their privacy policies for more information.
